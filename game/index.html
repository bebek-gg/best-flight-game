SKYWARD RUN

Three sorties over the river valley. Fly the rings, race the canyon, bring her home.

HOW TO FLY

W / S or ↑ / ↓ — pitch up / down  ·  A / D or ← / → — bank & turn

Shift — throttle up  ·  Ctrl / F — throttle down  ·  hold left mouse — steer by pointer

P — pause  ·  R — restart  ·  M — music on / off  ·  C — cockpit / chase camera  ·  Enter — start

SORTIES

1 · Ring Run — take off and fly through 5 sky rings.

2 · Canyon Dash — low-level river run against the clock. Low passes pay extra.

3 · Homecoming — land on the runway, gentle and on the centreline.
